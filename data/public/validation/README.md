# Independent public validation cohorts

This registry records public GEO datasets selected to contextualize the CALR molecular analysis.

- **GSE156336** is the primary exploratory RNA-seq cohort: 18 ET patients and 4 healthy controls, including CALR-mutated, JAK2V617F and triple-negative groups.
- **GSE103237** provides an independent expression cohort containing 7 CALR-mutated ET and 17 JAK2V617F ET cases within a larger MPN series, with 15 normal donor samples represented through the related normal-donor dataset.
- **GSE54644** is used for broader MPN/JAK-STAT pathway context and is not treated as a CALR-specific validation cohort.

These datasets are not bundled. Quantitative analyses must be performed from the original GEO files with accession and checksum provenance captured before inference.
