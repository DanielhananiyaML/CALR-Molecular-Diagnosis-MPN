# GSE156336 Sample Metadata Verification

## Verification status

The sample-to-group mapping used in the CALR public-cohort analysis was independently
checked against the NCBI GEO GSE156336 Series record.

The GEO Series record reports 22 samples comprising:
- 3 CALR-mutated ET samples
- 3 JAK2-mutated ET samples
- 12 triple-negative ET samples
- 4 healthy controls

The GEO sample ordering matches the matrix-column mapping used by this repository.
